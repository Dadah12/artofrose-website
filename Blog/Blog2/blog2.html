Bayanihan of Art: A Memorable Gallery Exhibit at Fishermall Malabon

Blog May 20 – June 5, 2024 • Fishermall Malabon Art Exhibit

Step inside a world where creativity, community, and Filipino talent shine! Our recent exhibit at Fishermall Malabon wasn’t just an art display—it was a celebration of local passion, teamwork, and on-the-spot magic.

A Gathering of Artists, A Gallery of Stories

There’s something magical about setting up a gallery with friends and fellow artists. Together, we transformed the event center into a vibrant space filled with portraits, landscapes, and unique masterpieces. Each wall told a new story.

We worked hand-in-hand, fixing frames, planning layouts, and making sure each artwork got its time to shine. It was real bayanihan—artists helping artists!

On-the-Spot Sketching: A Crowd Favorite

The exhibit wasn’t just for looking—it was interactive! Visitors lined up to have their portraits sketched live, bringing home a piece of the moment.

A Showcase of Local Masterpieces

From detailed charcoal portraits to vibrant paintings of everyday Filipino life, the gallery featured the best works of our co-artists.
